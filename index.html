Who We Are

Your Democratic neighbors
in West Norriton.

The West Norriton Democratic Committee is made up of residents who live, work, and raise families right here in the township. We're not a distant organization — we're your neighbors, and we're committed to local government that works for everyone.

We recruit and support Democratic candidates for local office, register voters, organize volunteers, and work year-round to make West Norriton a place where every resident can thrive.

Want to meet us? We hold monthly meetings at the West Norriton Township Building. All residents are welcome — no experience or party registration required.
